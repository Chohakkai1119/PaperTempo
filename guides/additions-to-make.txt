PAPERTEMPO — ADDITIONS TO MAKE

1. Loop a passage (build first)
   - Let players choose a start and end bar, or select “Loop this passage” from a mistake in the results to retry that section immediately.
   - Use bars 8–9 of Morning Lines for the demo so the loop crosses the hands-free page turn.

2. Show mistakes on the sheet music
   - After a take, mark missed notes directly on the score. In Scored tempo mode, label matched notes that landed early or late.

3. Compare two takes
   - Keep the last two takes in the browser. Compare pitch accuracy and timing for the same bars at the same tempo so players can see whether the retry improved.

4. Add a Snowflake Cortex practice coach
   - Add a “What should I practise next?” button to the results panel.
   - Send bar numbers, missed notes and available timing offsets to a small backend. Have the backend call the Snowflake Cortex REST API and return one short drill with a target bar or range and a tempo. Keep microphone audio in the browser and the Snowflake credential on the backend.
   - Example drill: “Play bars 8–9 at half tempo three times. Keep the beat steady through the page turn and focus on the first note of bar 9.”
   - Add a “Try this drill” button that jumps to the suggested bar and sets the practice range and tempo.
   - Another Snowflake option is to store practice results in Snowflake and compare progress across sessions.

5. Add “Hear this passage”
   - Play the selected bars at the target tempo so the player can hear the rhythm before retrying.

6. Add a live music visualiser if entering Creative Tech
   - Turn played notes into an animated ink trail across the staff.
   - Recommended track: Retro Remix. Lead with how the hands-free page turn reimagines the paper score.
